03 / THE DETAILS THAT MATTER

Before anyone pays a deposit

01

Agree what “comfortable” means

These are low-intensity proposals, not a medical assessment. Agree a personal walking/standing limit, heat tolerance, stairs, and whether airport assistance would help. Seek appropriate clinician advice about long-haul travel, medication and insurance cover. Book a car on sightseeing days; keep the afternoon free.

02

Check passports and entry rules

Departure airport does not determine nationality. Irish, Swiss and British citizens currently have a 30-day visa-free policy only through 31 December 2026; do not assume it extends to June 2027. Check each traveller’s passport with the relevant Chinese visa centre before committing to non-refundable bookings. UK guidance calls for six months’ passport validity and two blank pages.

03

Get the booking sequence right

Compare open-jaw flights into Beijing and out of Shanghai with a Shanghai return plus rail. Hold cancellable hotels first. Train schedules and fares will be confirmed nearer departure via 12306; bring the same passport used to book. The Palace Museum does not sell same-day tickets; its current release is seven days ahead at 20:00 Beijing time.

04

Make payments and shopping easy

Set up Alipay or Weixin Pay with an overseas card before departure, carry a backup card and some yuan. Explore pre-owned designer resale at Shanghai’s H22/Huaihai area or ZZER; request independent authentication and inspect condition, provenance and return policy. “Dupes” may mean lookalikes or counterfeits: avoid fake logos and be mindful of customs rules when bringing purchases home. Allow shopping money separately.
